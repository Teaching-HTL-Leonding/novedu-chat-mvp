# Tutors overview

Output: teacher-docs/content/00-introduction/03-tutors-overview.md · order 3

Job: A teacher deciding whether a tutor fits their lesson. After this chapter they
understand what a tutor is for, what the student experience looks like, and how a
teacher shapes it, at the concept level. Building one (the YAML fields) is a later
chapter; link forward, do not configure here.

Cover:
- What a tutor is: a chat with an AI that follows the teacher's instructions, on a
  topic the teacher chooses.
- The student experience: an open chat, optional starter questions on the empty
  screen, back-and-forth help.
- How the teacher shapes it: by writing instructions (and optionally reusing named
  pieces of prompt), so the tutor can explain, ask guiding questions, and stay within
  the teacher's rules instead of doing the work for the student.
- When a tutor is the right kind: open-ended help and practice, not grading.

Get right:
- A tutor is configured by prompt, not trained. Keep it conceptual.
- Tutors default to anonymous (the app does not record who chatted). Mention lightly
  at most; the anonymity choice belongs to the sharing chapters.
