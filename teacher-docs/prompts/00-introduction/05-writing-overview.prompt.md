# Writing overview

Output: teacher-docs/content/00-introduction/05-writing-overview.md · order 5

Job: A teacher deciding whether a writing activity fits their lesson. After this
chapter they understand the split-screen draft plus AI coach, that the coach reads but
cannot edit, and that the student saves their own text. Concept level; building one is
a later chapter.

Cover:
- What a writing activity is: the student writes in an editor while an AI coach reads
  the draft and gives feedback.
- The key boundary: the coach can read the draft but cannot edit it; the student stays
  the author.
- The student experience: draft on one side, ask the coach for feedback on the other,
  save the finished text.
- How the teacher shapes it: writes the task and how the coach should help (advise,
  not rewrite), and can give a starter scaffold.
- When writing is the right kind: producing a text of one's own with guidance beside
  it, not grading and not open chat.

Get right:
- The coach cannot change the student's text, by design; do not imply it can.
- Writing records who wrote what by default, unlike tutors and quizzes, because saving
  needs an author. Mention lightly; detail lives in the sharing chapters.
