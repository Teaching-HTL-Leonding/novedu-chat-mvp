# Quizzes overview

Output: teacher-docs/content/00-introduction/04-quizzes-overview.md · order 4

Job: A teacher deciding whether a quiz fits their lesson. After this chapter they
understand what a quiz is for, what students experience, and how the teacher shapes
grading, at the concept level. Building one (the YAML fields) is a later chapter.

Cover:
- What a quiz is: open-ended questions students answer in their own words; the AI
  grades each answer against guidance the teacher writes and gives feedback.
- The student experience: read a question, type an answer, get a verdict and feedback,
  and optionally discuss the question afterwards. If the teacher allows it, a student
  can attach a photo of their work.
- How the teacher shapes it: writes the question students see and a separate grading
  guide the AI uses, which students never see.
- When a quiz is the right kind: checking understanding with feedback, not open chat.

Get right:
- Answers are open-ended and AI-graded, not multiple choice.
- The grading guidance is private to the teacher; students never see it. Keep the
  chapter conceptual, no field names.
- Quizzes default to anonymous; mention lightly, detail lives in the sharing chapters.
